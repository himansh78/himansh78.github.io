## Research interests

I’m interested in quantifying Earth processes using surface measurements and geophysical parameters, with a focus on **improving seismic hazard quantification**. I also enjoy applying modern computational methods (including machine learning) to geophysical problems. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

## Current work (PhD)

**Physics-based ground motion simulations and seismic risk assessment in rapidly urbanising environments**  
Supervision: Mark Naylor, John McCloskey (University of Edinburgh), Karim Tarbali & Carmine Galasso (UCL). :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

My PhD explores kinematic models used in ground motion simulation, investigates variability in underlying parameters, and identifies gaps and opportunities to improve model accuracy and precision through rigorous engineering validation—ultimately producing recommendations for using kinematic models in the physics-based simulation paradigm for seismic hazard and risk assessment. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}
